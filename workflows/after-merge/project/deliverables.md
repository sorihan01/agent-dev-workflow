# Deliverables log

One entry per finished piece of work, newest first, written once when it lands (merged and live), in the past tense, with no status words such as "pending" or "not live yet". Every merged pull request appears in an entry, or as a linked line in the entry it belongs to.

Format:
- Heading: `### YYYY-MM-DD · <repo> · <title, 60 characters max>`
- ❌ Broken: what was wrong, with evidence (`file:line`, ticket).
- ✅ Fix: what changed, with the PR link, and who merged and deployed it.
- 📈 Result: what was measured on production after the deploy, or `unknown`. Never an estimate.
